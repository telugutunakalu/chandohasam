# Sample poems — constrained decoding of Telugu padyams

Poems generated by two language models under exact metrical constraints: gaṇa, prāsa and yati
enforced during decoding on the metrical DAWG (`meter_engine/indic_meter_dawg`). Each document shows
**two metrically perfect poems for every meter (37) and every decoding strategy (3)**, with the prompt
and the model's probability of every chosen token.

| document | model | decoding |
|---|---|---|
| [gemma-4-E4B-it.md](gemma-4-E4B-it.md) | `google/gemma-4-E4B-it` (BF16) | autoregressive |
| [diffusiongemma-26B-A4B-it.md](diffusiongemma-26B-A4B-it.md) | `nvidia/diffusiongemma-26B-A4B-it-NVFP4` (experts decoded to BF16) | block diffusion, frozen validated prefix |

## The three strategies

* **Masking only** — the model's distribution restricted to the tokens that keep the poem completable
  in meter; a token is drawn from it.
* **Masking + backtracking** — the same, rewinding to an earlier checkpoint when the allowed set
  collapses.
* **Hybrid** — the same mask, then re-ranking that prefers allowed tokens which complete the line.

## The grids the samples come from

Each grid: 37 meters × 3 topics × 5 seeds × 3 strategies = 1,665 poems, the same prompts for both
models. Every poem of both grids is complete and accepted by the repository's engines (strict
profile). Figures over each strategy's 555 poems:

| | E4B masking | E4B backtracking | E4B hybrid | Diffusion masking | Diffusion backtracking | Diffusion hybrid |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| in meter (engines, strict) | 100% | 100% | 100% | 100% | 100% | 100% |
| mean log-probability of the chosen token | −2.18 | −2.29 | −2.34 | −3.84 | −3.13 | −3.49 |
| model's first choice not allowed | 31% | 35% | 32% | 47% | 36% | 47% |
| single-akshara words (real verse: 4.0%) | 9.2% | 9.3% | 7.9% | 28.7% | 25.0% | 26.5% |
| poems repeating a line | 20.0% | 21.4% | 17.3% | 3.8% | 2.2% | 2.9% |

Without the constraint (same prompts, E4B) no poem was in meter (0 / 555).

## What "metrically perfect" means here

Complete, and accepted by the engines for gaṇa, prāsa and yati under the strict profile. Among those,
each document shows the two poems per meter and strategy that repeat no line and have the largest share
of real words (words of two or more aksharas found in the verse corpora in `dataset/`), then the
highest mean token log-probability.

Metrical correctness is guaranteed; linguistic quality is not. Most words in these poems are not real
Telugu words — the constraint decides where the model's preferred continuation does not fit the meter,
most often at yati and prāsa positions. The per-poem figures ("corpus words", "constraint overrode")
show how much.

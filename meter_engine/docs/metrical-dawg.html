One metre, whole

A vritta is a single path

A వృత్తము fixes every akshara. There is no choice anywhere in the line, so శార్దూలవిక్రీడితము's whole language is one string of 19 weights, and its slot automaton is a corridor: 20 states, 19 edges, one path. The యతి falls on the first akshara of the fifth gaṇa.

A fair question

Is it just a tree?

Near the start it behaves like one: 1,024 of the 1,431 states have exactly one way in. But 406 states — 28% of the machine — can be reached by more than one reading, and one state has 34 ways in. A tree of 1,431 nodes has 1,430 edges. This has 2,514, so 1,084 edges arrive at a state that already had a parent. That is the whole trick, and it is what a tree cannot do.

Three is not the ceiling either. Going out, a state has at most two edges, because the alphabet is only U and I: 1,117 states have both, 280 have one, and 34 are dead ends where a pāda can only finish. Going in, nothing caps it. The busiest state in the machine is reached from 34 different states.

Where the walk stops

What the machine will not tell you

  • Sound, at the యతి. The DAWG locates akshara 13; whether చిం agrees with శ్రీ is the yati engine's verdict. Here it does, under YATI-VY-12, and so do the other three pādas.
  • ప్రాస. The second-akshara rhyme is checked separately: కై, కా, క, కం, a క-prāsa across the four pādas.
  • The scansion itself. The machine trusts the U and I it is handed — which is why the pādānta laghu on pāda 1 is recorded in the report rather than quietly assumed.
  • Meaning. A perfectly metrical line of nonsense walks the automaton exactly like Pōtana.
